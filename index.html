  • 20 demo trades ek chhota sample hai — luck se accha result aa sakta hai. Isse yeh proof nahi hota ki strategy real me chalegi (neeche ki maths dekho).

1 · Framework — kya dekhna hai

EMA 20

Short-term trend. Price iske upar/niche hone se recent momentum ka idea milta hai.

EMA 50

Main trend. EMA 20 iske upar = bullish structure, niche = bearish structure.

RSI 14

Momentum. 50 ke aas-paas (45–55) = weak/sideways. Upar = up-momentum, niche = down.

Volume

Spike = volatility/breakout ho sakta hai — par har spike reliable nahi. Confirmation ke liye.

Golden rule: Trend tabhi "strong" jab price dono EMAs ki ek hi side ho aur EMAs clearly slope kar rahe hon. EMAs flat + price unhe baar-baar cross kare = sideways = skip.

2 · Reference — strong setups & avoid conditions

Yeh conditions batati hain ki analysis kab zyada strong hoti hai. Yeh guaranteed entry signals nahi hain — sab align hone par bhi outcome guaranteed nahi hota. Inhi conditions ko neeche wali checklist (section 3) verify karti hai. 2-min expiry sochne se pehle: pehle demo par journal karo.

📈 Strong bullish analysis · UP context

  • Price EMA 20 aur EMA 50 dono ke upar.
  • EMA 20, EMA 50 ke upar.
  • Dono EMAs upward slope me.
  • RSI 50 se upar, momentum improve karta hua.
  • Price kisi support area se bounce dikhaye.
  • Market sideways na ho.

📉 Strong bearish analysis · DOWN context

  • Price EMA 20 aur EMA 50 dono ke niche.
  • EMA 20, EMA 50 ke niche.
  • Dono EMAs downward slope me.
  • RSI 50 se niche.
  • Price kisi resistance area se reject ho.
  • Market sideways na ho.

🚫 Trade avoid karne ke signs — in me skip behtar

  • EMAs flat hain.
  • Price EMAs ko baar-baar cross kar raha.
  • RSI lagbhag 50 ke aas-paas (45–55).
  • Candles chhoti aur overlapping.
  • High-impact news ke aas-paas.

Yaad rahe: yeh analysis-strength ke signs hain, "abhi trade karo" wale signals nahi. Koi accuracy % nahi — kyunki 2-min binary par koi bharosemand accuracy hoti hi nahi. Sab align ho tab bhi outcome guaranteed nahi.

6 · Seekhne ke liye (yahi long-term me kaam aata hai)

Trend kaise identify karein?

Uptrend: price higher-highs & higher-lows bana raha ho, EMA 20 > EMA 50, dono up-slope. Downtrend iska ulta. Agar EMAs aapas me chipke hain aur price dono ko baar-baar cross kar raha hai — wo trend nahi, range hai.

False breakout kaise pehchaanein?

Breakout candle ke turant baad agar price wapas level ke andar aa jaye (close back inside), aur volume follow-through na de — wo aksar fakeout hota hai. Ek confirmation candle ka wait karna helpful hota hai.

Support/Resistance + EMA + RSI kaise combine karein?

Sabse "clean" observations tab hote hain jab teeno align karein: price trend-side EMA par pullback kare, ek known S/R level se react kare, aur RSI trend direction confirm kare (uptrend me RSI 50 ke upar rehta hai). Ek indicator akela kaafi nahi.

Backtesting & journaling — sabse important skill

Isi tool ka journal use karo. 20 nahi, 100+ trades ka data chahiye kisi bhi conclusion ke liye. Har trade ka setup + result likho, phir dekho kaunse setups me tumhara win-rate break-even se upar hai — aur kaunse me tum sirf luck par the.

Kab bilkul trade NA karein
  • Sideways market (flat EMAs, price cross kar raha).
  • RSI 45–55 (weak momentum).
  • High-impact news ke aas-paas.
  • Low-liquidity / off-hours.
  • Jab "revenge" me ho ya bore ho ke trade kar rahe ho.
